Privacy Policy

PipDesk for Android · Last updated 12 November 2026

In short: PipDesk collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics and no server, and it works with the internet switched off.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — account settings, challenge rules, trades and notes — is saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage. None of it is sent anywhere, and no one else can read it.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-up and no user account.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking software of any kind.
  • No network requests. The app runs fully offline.
  • No connection to your broker, bank or any trading account.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

CSV import

When you import a CSV file of your trade history, the file is read on your device and turned into journal entries stored on that device. The file is never uploaded. The app can only read a file you pick yourself.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset all data erases everything the app has stored. Uninstalling the app removes it too. Because nothing is sent anywhere, there is no copy for us to delete.

Children

PipDesk is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
